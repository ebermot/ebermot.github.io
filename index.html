Quantum computing · Research & adoption

Elie Bermot

I am a Quantum Enablement and Adoption Manager at Pasqal, where I help users make the most of neutral-atom quantum computing technology.

I completed my PhD in October 2026, jointly at IRIF (Université Paris Cité) and Pasqal. My research focused on quantum algorithms for combinatorial optimization, particularly quantum annealing and tunneling.

Interests: Quantum algorithms, quantum optimization, adiabatic quantum computing, and quantum simulation.
